# n8n Automation Portfolio

A set of industry-agnostic business automations, each demonstrated for a fictional client, built to show prospective clients (and employers) that any business can be automated.

## Language

**Automation**:
One self-contained business process that runs without a human, built generically so it fits any business once given a business profile.
_Avoid_: Bot, flow, script

**Business profile**:
The small set of facts that adapts an automation to one business: name, services, tone, prices, and similar details.
_Avoid_: Config, settings block, template variables

**Demo client**:
A fictional business that an automation is shown working for. Always labelled as fictional, never presented as a real customer.
_Avoid_: Client (alone), customer, fake company

**Industry swap**:
Re-running the same automation with a different business profile to show it fitting another industry.
_Avoid_: Reskin, re-theme

**Tier**:
The difficulty level of an automation in the portfolio: simple, medium, or advanced.
_Avoid_: Level, stage

**Case study**:
The portfolio write-up of one automation: the problem, how it works, and the result, with its demo.
_Avoid_: Project page, writeup
