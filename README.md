# Any business can be automated

AI automations by **DarkLvrd** · Dark Century Studios · [darklvrd.github.io](https://darklvrd.github.io) · [@DarkLvrd](https://x.com/DarkLvrd)

Each project is a working n8n automation, shown running for a **fictional demo client** and built so it can be switched to any business by changing one "business profile" box.

## Try them yourself

| Automation | What to do | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **M1 Prospect researcher** | [Open the form](https://darklvrd.app.n8n.cloud/form/corvell-try) and paste any small business website | About 30 seconds later you see a brief on the company and a personal first email written for it |
| **S1 Lead catcher** | [Watch the 30-second demo](s1-lead-catcher/demo.mp4) | A patient enquiry is rated, saved to the CRM, answered and flagged in under 9 seconds |
